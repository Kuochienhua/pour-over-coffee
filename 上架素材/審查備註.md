# App 審查備註（事先準備，避免 Guideline 2.1 要求補件）

「生活手繪」第一次送審時被要求補「真機操作影片」和用途說明（Guideline 2.1）。
這次事先放進「App 審查資訊 → 備註」，並在「附件」上傳影片，減少來回。

## 送審前你要做的事

1. **用真的 iPhone 錄操作影片**（控制中心 → 螢幕錄影），約 1–2 分鐘，從打開 App 開始，依序示範：
   - 「比例」頁：換沖煮方法、調粉量與粉水比、選濾杯
   - 「計時」頁：按開始，等第一段語音提示，再回到桌面看動態島
   - 按「完成」→ 勾選口感 → 儲存 → 看「風味診斷」→ 套用到下一杯
   - 「豆子」頁新增一支豆子；「紀錄」頁看一筆紀錄、按「再沖一次」
   - 「比例」頁右上角 QR Code 分享
2. 影片存成 `上架素材/審查影片/手沖咖啡小幫手-操作示範.mp4`，送審時上傳到「附件」。

## 要貼到「備註」的內容（英文，直接複製）

Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing 手沖咖啡小幫手 (Pour-Over Helper). A screen recording captured on a physical iPhone is attached.

**Purpose and audience**
A brewing companion for home pour-over coffee hobbyists. It calculates coffee-to-water ratios, guides each pour with a timer, voice and haptic cues, and after tasting suggests what to adjust next time (grind, temperature, ratio).

**How to test (no login, no setup)**
1. "比例" (Ratio) tab: pick a brew method under "沖煮方法", adjust dose and ratio. The pour plan at the bottom updates live.
2. "計時" (Timer) tab: tap "開始" (Start). A spoken cue plays 3 seconds before each pour, a progress bar shows the target weight, and the timer also appears in the Dynamic Island / Lock Screen as a Live Activity.
3. Tap "完成" (Finish), select taste notes such as "尖酸" (sour) and save. The "風味診斷" (Taste diagnosis) screen suggests adjustments; tap the button at the bottom to apply them to the next brew.
4. "豆子" (Beans) tab: tap + to add a coffee bean. "紀錄" (Log) tab: open a log or swipe right to brew it again.
5. The QR-code button at the top right of the Ratio tab shares the recipe as a pourover:// link.

**Bluetooth scale (optional, experimental)**
In "設定" (Settings) the user may connect a Bluetooth scale that implements the standard Bluetooth SIG Weight Scale Service (0x181D). Bluetooth permission is requested only when the user taps "搜尋藍牙秤" (Search for scale). All other features work fully without a scale.

**Data and external services**
None. The app makes no network requests and uses no third-party SDKs, analytics, ads, accounts or payments. All data (brew logs, beans, photos chosen with the system photo picker) is stored on-device with SwiftData. Voice cues use on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer.

**Regional differences**
None. The interface is in Traditional Chinese.

Thank you!
